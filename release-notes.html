Version 1.5 Latest

A polish update with a new Appearance setting, tidier Settings and a round of fixes.

New

  • Appearance: choose System, Light or Dark in Settings. Your choice applies across the app, including the unlock screen and onboarding.
  • Reorganized Settings: grouped into Appearance, Reminders, Mood tray, Tiles, Purchase, Data and About. About now shows your app version.
  • Reminders shortcut: if notifications are turned off for VibeTiles, Settings shows a link that opens iPhone Settings.
  • Haptics: a gentle success tap when a mood is placed or logged.
  • VoiceOver: days in Month view read out the date and the moods logged, mood choices in the editor are proper buttons that show which one is selected, and VoiceOver announces what you just logged.
  • Delete confirmations: VibeTiles now asks before deleting a mood or a plan.

Fixes

  • Daylight saving: on the days the clocks change, logging, dropping tiles, hour labels, block positions, the fade line and the daily reminder now use the correct time. Before, they could be an hour off.
  • Purchase and restore: clearer messages when a purchase is waiting for approval (Ask to Buy), when no earlier purchase is found on restore, and when the unlock isn’t available yet. After the free trial, the unlock screen now opens reliably when you try to add a mood from the time picker or a plan’s “How did it feel?” prompt.
  • Stability: deleting a plan no longer risks a crash, search handles unusual dates and times more safely, your latest changes are saved when the app goes to the background, and “Delete all data” now clears every screen right away.
  • Dark mode: the VibeTiles wordmark in onboarding is now easy to read.

Your data handling hasn’t changed: your moods stay on your iPhone, reminders are scheduled on your iPhone, export is still CSV, and the one-time $4.99 unlock is the same. See the Privacy Policy.

Version 1.0

The first version of VibeTiles: drag color mood tiles onto your day, stretch moods across time, plan future moods (shown outlined), see day, week and month views, search your moods, get an optional daily reminder with a rotating quote, and export to CSV. Free for 30 days, then a one-time $4.99 unlock. No subscription.
