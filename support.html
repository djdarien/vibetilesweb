FAQ

How do I log a mood?

Drag one of the 12 color mood tiles onto your day. Blocks start at 1 hour and can be adjusted in half-hour steps, and a mood can span as much time as you need.

Can I plan moods for later?

Yes. Moods placed in the future appear outlined so you can tell plans apart from what already happened.

What views are there?

Day, week and month views. The day view is laid out hour by hour.

How does search work?

Search opens on today, and you can look back through your entries from there.

Can I get a reminder?

Yes. You can turn on a daily reminder notification, which includes a rotating quote. You can change the time or turn it off in Settings, or in iOS Settings.

How much does VibeTiles cost?

It's free for 30 days. After that, a one-time $4.99 in-app purchase unlocks it for good. There is no subscription.

What happens when the free trial ends?

You can still view, edit, delete and export everything you've logged. Adding new moods requires the one-time unlock.

I reinstalled or got a new phone. How do I get my unlock back?

Use “Restore purchase” in Settings in the app, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone. VibeTiles has no accounts and doesn't sync your moods to our servers. See our Privacy Policy.

Can I export my data?

Yes, you can export your entries as a CSV file from Settings.

Is VibeTiles a medical app?

No. VibeTiles is a personal journaling tool for noting how your day feels. It isn't intended to diagnose or treat anything.
