VibeTiles is a colorful mood tracker for iPhone from Good Vibez.

Features

  • Drag & drop moods. Drag color mood tiles straight onto your day.
  • Flexible blocks. 1-hour blocks you can adjust in half-hour steps.
  • Moods that span time. Stretch a mood across as much of the day as it lasted.
  • Plan ahead. Plan future moods — they show as outlined tiles.
  • Day, week & month views. Including an hourly day view.
  • Search. Search opens on today so you start where you are.
  • Daily reminder. An optional daily notification with a rotating quote.
  • 12 mood tiles. A palette of 12 colorful moods.
  • Fair pricing. Free for 30 days, then a one-time $4.99 unlock. No subscription.
