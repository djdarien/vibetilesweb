Version 1.5

What’s new in v1.5

A polish update: a new Appearance setting, tidier Settings, and lots of fixes.

Light, Dark or System

A new Appearance setting. Pick Light or Dark, or follow your iPhone. It applies across the whole app.

Tidier Settings

Settings are regrouped into Appearance, Reminders, Mood tray, Tiles, Purchase, Data and About. About now shows your app version.

Right on time, all year

Times stay on the right hour on the days the clocks change for daylight saving.

Think before you delete

VibeTiles now asks before deleting a mood or a plan.

A tap you can feel

A gentle haptic tap when a mood lands on your day.

Better with VoiceOver

Month days read out their date and moods, mood choices work as buttons, and VoiceOver tells you what you just logged.

Plus clearer messages when you buy or restore the unlock, and stability fixes throughout. Your data still stays on your iPhone.
