Features

Everything VibeTiles does

A simple, colorful way to note how your day feels, one tile at a time.

01 · Drag & drop

Drag color mood tiles onto your day

Pick a tile from the palette at the bottom of the screen and drag it onto any hour. That’s the whole idea: no forms, no typing, just color on a timeline.

02 · Time blocks

1-hour blocks, adjustable in half-hour steps

Each mood starts as a 1-hour block. Make it longer or shorter in half-hour steps so it fits how your day actually went.

03 · Spans

Moods can span time

A calm morning or a stressful afternoon rarely fits into one hour. Stretch a mood across as much of the day as it lasted.

04 · Planning

Plan future moods, shown outlined

Place moods on hours that haven’t happened yet to plan how you’d like to feel. Planned moods appear outlined, so plans and what really happened are easy to tell apart.

05 · Views

Day, week and month views

The day view is laid out hour by hour. Switch to week or month to see your moods across a longer stretch of time.

06 · Search

Search that opens on today

Search starts on today, so you begin where you are. From there, find any mood you’ve logged.

07 · Reminder

A daily reminder with a rotating quote

Turn on an optional daily reminder at a time you choose. Each one comes with a quote from a rotating collection. You can turn it off anytime in Settings.

08 · Mood tiles

12 mood tiles

Happy, excited, loved, calm, bored, tired, sleep, sad, anxious, grumpy, jealous and angry: a palette of 12 colorful tiles designed for VibeTiles.

09 · Privacy & export

On-device privacy and CSV export

Your moods are stored on your iPhone. There’s no account and no analytics, and nothing is synced to our servers. Want your data elsewhere? Export your entries as a CSV file from Settings. Read the Privacy Policy.
