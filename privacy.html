Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy explains how VibeTiles, an iPhone app published by Good Vibez, handles your information. In short: your moods stay on your device. We don't collect, track or sell your data.

Data stored on your device

  • Mood entries, plans and quotes are saved locally on your iPhone with Apple's SwiftData framework. The app doesn't use iCloud/CloudKit sync and doesn't send them to us or anyone else.
  • Your settings (like reminder time, default view and display preferences) are stored locally in the app's preferences.
  • Free trial start date is stored in your device's Keychain so the trial isn't reset by reinstalling the app. It stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. VibeTiles has no accounts, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. The app doesn't make network requests to our servers. Its privacy manifest says no data is collected and no tracking is done.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. Apple processes the payment. The app only checks with Apple whether you own the unlock, and we never get your payment details. Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for permission to send notifications. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device (they aren't push notifications from a server). You can turn them off anytime in the app or in iOS Settings.

Exporting your data

You can export your entries as a CSV file. The file is only shared where you choose to send it through the iOS share sheet.

Deleting your data

You can delete entries in the app. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data from your device. The trial date in the Keychain may stay on the device so the trial can't be restarted.

Children

VibeTiles doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the updated version on this page.
